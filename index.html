About Me

I began my career in scientific research, studying complex natural systems and using data to answer meaningful questions. That experience sparked a deeper interest in analytics and ultimately led me to pursue an M.S. in Data Science.

Today, I combine a researcher’s curiosity with the technical skills of a data scientist, using statistical modeling, machine learning, and visualization to uncover patterns and support data-driven decision-making.

My work spans environmental data, renewable energy, operational analytics, and predictive modeling, but the goal remains the same: find the signal, understand the story, and communicate it clearly.
